## Sources cross-referenced

The framework's controls are original work. The mapping files cite other publications by clause, item or paragraph
number so that users can see how the controls correspond. They do not reproduce those publications. Each mapping
is the author's own interpretation. No source owner has endorsed it.

- **UK Government publications** (GDS AI Playbook for the UK Government; Algorithmic Transparency Recording Standard;
  Code of Practice for the Cyber Security of AI; JSP 936 Part 1; Data and AI Ethics Framework and Self-Assessment
  Tool). Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Where mapping labels
  quote these sources, they do so under that licence. No official status or endorsement is implied.
- **NIST AI 100-1 (AI Risk Management Framework 1.0) and NIST AI 600-1.** National Institute of Standards and
  Technology, U.S. Department of Commerce. Referenced by subcategory and risk identifier. Republished courtesy of the
  National Institute of Standards and Technology.
- **Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (EU AI Act).** © European Union, https://eur-lex.europa.eu. Referenced by article.
  Only European Union legislation published in the Official Journal of the European Union is deemed authentic.
- **ISO/IEC 42001:2023.** Referenced by clause number only. Labels are the author's own descriptions, not ISO text.
  ISO/IEC 42001 is copyright ISO/IEC and is available from ISO and national standards bodies.
- **MITRE AI Maturity Model and Organizational Assessment Tool Guide** (2023, © 2022 MITRE, Approved for Public
  Release, Distribution Unlimited, case 22-1879). Referenced by pillar and dimension; dimension names are cited as
  names only, with descriptions in the author's own words.
- **UNESCO, AI Maturity Framework: a self-positioning guide for public administrations** (2025). Licensed by UNESCO
  under CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO. Referenced by pillar and category. Category names are cited as names only, with
  descriptions in the author's own words. No UNESCO endorsement is implied.
- **CSA AI Controls Matrix (AICM).** Not included in this release. A cross-reference will be added only with the
  Cloud Security Alliance's permission.
