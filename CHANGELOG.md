# Changelog

Changes to the framework: controls, criteria, overlays, levels, mappings and the method documents. Versions follow
MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH as set out in CONTRIBUTING.md.

From 12 November 2026 the website at https://aacf.adriansaxton.com is released separately and does not change the
framework version. Versions 1.0.1, 1.1.1, 1.1.2 and 1.1.3 below changed only the website and repository; they are
kept as published.

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-10)

Minor release: three new Level 3 criteria, so Level 3 asks for more on three controls (443 criteria in all).

- **New criteria.** MON-04.4: people affected by an AI incident that is not serious are told what happened.
  OPM-10.6: research and evaluation plans manage risks to researchers' safety as well as participants'. GOV-10.4
  (generative AI): review before release checks for reproduced protected work and copied likeness or voice.
- **Choose the standards you answer to.** The self-assessment, the workbook (Profile and a new Standards sheet) and
  the report show, for each standard you select, which mapped items have their foundation in place: every control
  mapped directly to the item at Level 3 or above. This is readiness, not compliance. All standards are selected by
  default.
- **Mapping provenance.** Each cross-reference file records who mapped it, who reviewed it and when it was checked,
  and every item carries a confidence rating (high, medium or low) with a reason below high. Shown on each standard's
  page, in the matrix and in the CSV and JSON.
- **Mappings re-checked** against the controls split out in 1.0.0 (GOV-10, MON-09, OPM-10, SEC-06) and the five
  agentic criteria. Some items gained supporting controls; some direct ratings were lowered to partial where the
  control's wording is general (for example EU AI Act Art 25 and Art 26(12), four ATRS scope items); three JSP 936
  defence-specific items are now marked out of scope.
- The Standards page no longer claims every item is covered when some are not; hover an out-of-scope count for the
  reason.
- **What this framework does not cover:** a new page and reference-guide section listing residual gaps and emerging
  risks (agentic AI beyond the first criteria, intellectual property, live red-teaming, effects on jobs and others)
  and what is left to other frameworks by design.
- **Downloads:** the reference guide is now a PDF with a cover and contents; the workbooks have a consistent house
  style; the controls matrix opens with All controls and a new All criteria sheet for filtering.
- Overlays page laid out three by two; the Method page has a contents list.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-09)

First public release.

- 93 controls in 11 domains, each with numbered Level 3 criteria (440 in total) tagged core or by overlay.
- Six overlays: public body, provides AI to others, regulated or high-impact use, generative AI, agentic AI, builds or
  tunes its own models.
- Assessor rulebook and intended-use statement; Level 0 means confirmed absence; results reported against the
  Level 3 foundation and against agreed targets separately.
- Cross-references to eleven sources (CSA AICM held back pending permission).
- Assessment workbook, report generator, website with self-assessment, and a fictional worked example.
- Revised after an independent review of draft 0.1.0.
